FAQ

Find the answers to the questions families ask us most often below.

If my child is around SKY university level, can they apply to Russell Group universities?

A strong SKY-level academic profile can provide a strong starting point for UK applications, but UK universities assess students according to their own course-specific requirements. We help families understand where that profile is genuinely competitive and where additional preparation may be needed.

Is UK study really a better choice than the US?

UK universities have a strong reputation with Korean and international employers, while the shorter UK bachelor's degree can reduce the total cost and time compared with many four-year systems.

My child is in middle school — is that too early to start?

It is never too early — the families who achieve the strongest results are those who begin building the academic profile, English foundations, and extracurricular direction in middle school, long before the application itself becomes urgent.

You mentioned you workon behalf of a limited number of students — do you currently have availability?

We keep a small number of places open each intake, but availability changes quickly — we recommend making an initial enquiry as early as possible to avoid disappointment, and we will always be honest with you about our current capacity.

What about employment after a UK degree — do Korean companies recognise it?

Russell Group degrees are well recognised by Korea's major employers across finance, consulting, engineering, and the professions. Also, eligible graduates may be able to remain in the UK after graduation under the Graduate Route, subject to the immigration rules in force at the time.

You say you handle everything personally — can we really expect better results than a large consultancy?

Large consultancies assign junior coordinators and follow standardised templates used across hundreds of students — we work exclusively with a limited number of families each year, and every piece of advice, every personal statement, and every strategy is built specifically around your child and no one else's.

Top destinations

Oxford

Oxford's weekly one-on-one tutorials produce a different kind of graduate — one trained to argue, defend, and think under direct pressure from a subject expert. Strongest for law, medicine, politics, economics, and the humanities, its degree remains the single most recognised overseas qualification among Korean employers. Entry is among the most competitive in the world, but for the right student, no credential travels further.

Cambridge

Where Oxford rewards argument, Cambridge rewards discovery — it is the natural home for students drawn to mathematics, engineering, and the sciences, and its Tripos system pushes analytical rigour harder than any other undergraduate programme in the world. Students work alongside academics who are actively shaping their fields, not just teaching them. For careers in deep tech, biomedical research, or quantitative finance, there is no better starting point.

Imperial

The only UK university entirely devoted to science, engineering, medicine, and business, Imperial's singular focus means every resource and industry connection points in one direction. Graduates move directly into roles at companies that actively recruit from campus — particularly in technology, energy, and finance — making its employment outcomes among the strongest of any university in the world. For Korean students targeting technical careers, the practical advantage goes well beyond its league table position.

UCL

UCL operates more like a global research institution than a traditional university — diverse, sprawling, and deeply embedded in London's professional and creative life. It leads internationally in architecture (The Bartlett is consistently ranked the world's finest), law, biomedicine, and the social sciences. Its central London location means students build professional networks from day one, an advantage that compounds quickly after graduation.

King's College London

Ranked among the world's top 40 universities and embedded in the heart of London, King's has built an exceptional reputation in biomedical sciences, law, nursing, and the humanities. Its location — minutes from the Houses of Parliament, the financial district, and some of the world's leading hospitals — means students don't just study their field, they live inside it. For Korean students, King's combination of academic rigour and London's professional ecosystem consistently produces graduates who are competitive both internationally and in the Korean market.

Durham

England's third-oldest university sits inside a UNESCO World Heritage city, its collegiate structure offering an experience closer to Oxbridge than almost anywhere else in the country. Consistently ranked among the UK's top ten, Durham excels in law, social sciences, business, and the humanities, and its graduates are sought after by employers who understand that the university punches considerably above its public profile. For Korean families who want the prestige, the environment, and the three-year degree — without the extreme selectivity of Oxford or Cambridge — Durham deserves to be near the top of every shortlist.
